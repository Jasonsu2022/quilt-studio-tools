About Quilt Studio Tools

Fast, readable, distraction-free calculators for quilters.

Quilt Studio Tools builds practical, mobile-first calculators designed to be easy to read at the fabric shop counter and reliable at the cutting mat.

Our Quilt Binding Calculator supports Imperial, Metric, and Mixed units, straight-of-grain and continuous bias cutting, and one-tap printable cutting cards.

Our Quilt Backing & Batting Calculator figures backing fabric yardage for 42″–44″ quilting cotton or 108″ wideback, compares vertical vs. horizontal panel piecing with a live layout diagram, and matches standard pre-packaged batting sizes.
